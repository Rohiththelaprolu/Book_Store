About BookHive

A small online bookstore built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

📚 Our Mission

Make discovering good books simple, affordable and enjoyable for Indian readers.

🇮🇳 Indian Context

We highlight Indian authors, local pricing in rupees and familiar categories.

💻 Built to Learn

BookHive demonstrates semantic HTML, CSS Flexbox/Grid and JavaScript DOM and localStorage skills.

What We Offer

  • Featured books and categories
  • Search, category filtering and sorting
  • Individual book details and customer reviews
  • Persistent shopping cart with quantity controls
  • Responsive mobile, tablet and desktop layouts
